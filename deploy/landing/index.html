01/Built for privacy

Built for privacy.

Zero-knowledge by architecture, not by promise.
The server cannot read your messages — it does not have the keys.

End-to-end encrypted

AES-256-GCM with Double Ratchet forward secrecy. Each message uses a fresh key. Compromise one — the rest stay sealed.

Zero accounts

No phone, no email, no signup. The app does not know who you are because it does not ask.

Peer-to-peer

Messages travel directly between devices over WebRTC. The relay only helps you find each other — never your content.

Auto-delete

Messages disappear after five minutes by default. Voice notes, files, attachments — gone with them.

02/How it works

Three steps.

From install to private conversation in under thirty seconds.
No onboarding. No verification. No friction.

01

Create an encrypted room

One tap. The app generates a 384-bit room ID and a fresh ephemeral keypair.

02

Share the link

Send it however you trust — Signal, in person, a folded note. Single use, ten-minute expiry.

03

Talk privately

Direct peer-to-peer connection. Close the app and the room is gone. No history, no trace.
